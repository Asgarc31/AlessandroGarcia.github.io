## Overview
This diagram shows the circuit that involves a force sensor, voltage regulator, op-amp, an LED, and the PIC nano provided by the class. These parts have been selected because they have already been tested in class, meaning I have some prior experience with them. As for the type of sensor, I decided on an IR sensor because it is useful when measuring shorter distances. The circuit will be supplied with a 5V power supply and will also be connected both the circuit with the motion sensor and the one with a force sensor as they will need to work in conjunction to meet our standards for the trash cans alert system.
